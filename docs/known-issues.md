# MuJoCo: what users and the documentation already report

Gathered 15 October 2026, before the deep dive's registered studies, to aim the first tests. Every item is a report,
not a measurement of ours, unless the last column says it was measured; each links to its source. Where we have
measured an item, our own number is the one that counts.

These reports and Senua AI's findings overlap: where they are the same departure, [the issues](issues.md) counts it once.

## What users end up tuning

Elliptic cone, high impratio, Newton solver, harder solref and solimp, optional noslip, multiccd ([2503.02738](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.02738)); friction, masses, centres of mass, reflected inertia and gains randomised ([MuJoCo Playground](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.08844)); fitted contact stiffness, damping and friction per system to real impacts ([Acosta et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.00541)).

## The first tests this points at

1. The hold and a two-pad grip, at loads across the cone, under the defaults and the recommended options, at 0.5 to
   4 ms; an incline.
2. The slide at 0, 22.5 and 45 degrees to the pyramidal cone's axes: distance, sideways drift, lift.
3. The bounce at steps from 0.25 to 10 ms, past refsafe's clamp; resting depth.
4. A free tumbling body and a multi-link arm under each integrator.
5. Convergence, a resting stack, face-to-face boxes.
